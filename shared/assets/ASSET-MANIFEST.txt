Packaged assets (all original, abstract, without text, logos or emblems):
- heroes/<preset>-960.webp, heroes/<preset>-1600.webp — abstract preset hero backgrounds for
  zamrud-khatulistiwa, bahari-nusantara, batik-sogan, merah-putih and tenun-senja.
- ornaments/<presetId>.svg — repeating decorative tile per preset (leaves, waves, repeating
  geometry, simple bands, crossing threads), generated by scripts/make-visuals.py.
- covers/<presetId>.svg — text-free fallback cover for issues without an uploaded cover,
  generated by scripts/make-visuals.py.

Not packaged:
- default-cover.svg — superseded by covers/; kept in the source tree as a text-free reference.

NusantaraReborn bundles no university logo, state emblem, institutional favicon or photograph.
Journal logos, favicons and homepage images come only from the journal's own OJS configuration.
